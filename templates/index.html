● Main model: Logistic Regression

Model performance

Four algorithms were trained and tuned with 5-fold cross-validation on 57,452 emails and tested on 24,623 unseen emails. Logistic Regression is the main model: it is nearly as accurate as the best model and, unlike SVM, it gives a probability and a clear explanation for every prediction.

● Pipeline

How it works

Every email goes through the same five steps that were used to train the model in the notebook.

1

Clean the text

Lowercase the text, replace links and email addresses with placeholder tokens, and remove numbers and symbols.

http://bank.com → urltoken
2

Remove noise

Drop common stop words ("the", "and") and lemmatise words to their base form.

accounts → account
3

TF-IDF

Turn the text into numbers: how important each word and two-word phrase is in this email compared to all emails.

50,000 words & phrases
4

Chi-square selection

Keep only the features that best separate phishing from legitimate email.

50,000 → {{ "{:,}".format(n_features) }} features
5

Logistic Regression

Each feature has a learned weight. The weighted sum is turned into a probability, and 50% or more means phishing.

P = 1 / (1 + e−score)

Why Logistic Regression?

  • Explainable: every word has a weight, so the app can show why it made each decision.
  • Gives a probability: users see how confident the model is, not just yes or no.
  • Accurate: about 98% accuracy, within 0.2% of the best model tested.
  • Fast and light: predictions take milliseconds and the model file is only 40 KB.

Limitations

  • Text only: the model does not check sender addresses, headers, attachments or where links really lead.
  • Dataset age: much of the training data is from older public corpora (for example Enron, 2000s), so new phishing styles may be missed.
  • Dataset patterns: some words reflect the dataset rather than phishing itself (for example "enron" or "python" point to legitimate).
  • English only: emails in other languages will not be understood.
  • A helper, not a guarantee: always verify suspicious emails through an official channel.
